This is a real human on the page, not a LinkedIn cutout — and I want to name that first because it's rare. The hard lessons carry actual cost: the work addiction that 'took me down,' the sexual abuse rooted in trained compliance, the food-as-repression mechanism she chased for decades, the scarcity syndrome she confesses ran beneath her success until five or six years ago. What keeps it from the highest marks is that almost every failure has already been metabolized into wisdom by the time we meet it — the arc is always mistake-to-transformation, tied up. **Weaknesses:**
